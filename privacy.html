2. What data we collect: nothing on our servers

PomoGP has no backend, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. No personal data is transmitted to us, because there is no server to receive it. Everything the app remembers (timer settings, chosen team/driver/compound, selected track) is stored exclusively in your own browser via localStorage (key pomogp:v2) and never leaves your device. Deleting site data in your browser removes it permanently.
