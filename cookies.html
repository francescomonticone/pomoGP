Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Short version

PomoGP sets zero cookies — no first-party, no third-party, no analytics, no advertising. That is why there is no cookie banner on this site: there is nothing to consent to.

2. What we store instead: localStorage (on your device only)

To remember your setup between visits, the app uses the browser's localStorage, which — unlike cookies — is never sent over the network. Under the ePrivacy rules this counts as strictly necessary storage for a service you explicitly requested, so no consent is required.

KeyContentPurposeExpiry
pomogp:v2 Driver, team, compound, timer durations, laps, circuit, audio/notification flags Restore your setup on next visit Until you clear site data (no automatic expiry)

3. Third-party storage

Google Fonts (used as a typeface fallback) does not set cookies or use local storage in the browser. No other third party is involved.

4. How to erase it

Clear your browser's site data for this domain, or run localStorage.removeItem('pomogp:v2') in the developer console. The setup wizard will appear again on your next visit.
