# Brag Plan: PomoGP

## What is this app?
A Formula 1 Pomodoro focus timer: pick a team, driver and tyre compound, survive the start lights, and push through focus stints while a real circuit map fills red — with original F1 team radios.

## The angle
A race-weekend trailer for concentration. The joke that earns it: this is the most over-engineered Pomodoro timer ever built — five red lights just to start studying.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen. Five red dots blink on one by one with beeps. "LIGHTS OUT." Then the line: "Train your focus like a race weekend."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The setup flow: Ferrari team card with car photo → Leclerc driver slide → SOFT tyre card, arriving one by one.
- The working app: giant 25:00 countdown while the Monza outline fills red and the car dot runs the lap.
- The pit radio karaoke: "BOX, BOX, BOX." lighting up word by word under the equalizer.

## Outro / punchline
Checkered flag. "P1 · SESSION COMPLETE". URL: francescomonticone.github.io/pomoGP.

## User flow worth showing
Setup wizard (team → driver → tyre) → start lights → focus with live track → pit radio subtitles → P1 overlay. The centerpiece scenes show this flow, not marketing copy.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: F1 race-weekend trailer for a productivity timer
- Interpretation: wide shots, big condensed type, dramatic reveals, restrained but powerful motion; funny only through the premise itself.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0B0B0F (void black)
- Accent: #E10600 (F1 red)
- Text: #FFFFFF, dim #A7A7B3, pit yellow #FFF500
- Display font: Barlow Condensed 700/900 italic (fallback for proprietary Formula1 Display)
- Body font: Titillium Web
- Strongest visual element: the glowing red track outline + giant tabular countdown

## Share copy (draft)
PomoGP: an F1 Pomodoro timer with real circuits, start lights and original team radios. Lights out and away we focus. 🏁

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — driving bed under everything, real team-radio clips as featured moments
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (110 BPM, 60s, excerpt 0–20s, climax 18–20s under P1)
- Music treatment: full energy from 0, duck slightly under radio moments, hard end at 20s
- Music cue guidance: preset read; strong cues 18.01/18.55/20.19 — P1 reveal locked near 18.55; beat grid ~0.55s for sequential card arrivals (every other beat for readability)
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — track glow and title presence breathe with energy
- SFX posture: sparse, professional — 5 light beeps, 1 lights-out hit, 2 real radio clips (start jingle + "Box, box!")
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Hook lights — 5 beeps in sequence with dots
  - Setup cards — soft arrival ticks on the beat grid
  - Pit karaoke — real hamilton.mp3 "Box, box, box" with word-by-word lighting
  - P1 — lights-out-style hit + celebration clip
- Restraint rule: music never covers radio voices; SFX only on motion that exists

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Lights out hook — 3s (0–3)
Black void. Five red dots appear one by one (0.3/0.85/1.4/1.95/2.5s) with beeps. "LIGHTS OUT." slams in, then "Train your focus like a race weekend." holds.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 5 dots sequential.
Audio intent: rising tension, beeps then silence before music drops.
Audio-coupled idea: beep per dot.
Music: vol10 from 0s, full.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Setup flow — 5s (3–8)
Three cards arrive one by one on beat grid (~3.6/4.7/5.8s): Ferrari team card with car, Leclerc 16 driver slide, SOFT tyre card ("15 min focus"). Label above: "Pick your team. Pick your driver. Pick your poison."
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards sequential with soft ticks.
Audio intent: confident, building.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card arrival ticks.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Focus lap — 6s (8–14)
Giant "25:00" counting down fast (time-lapse feel), Monza outline filling red left-to-right, car dot running, S1/S2/S3 badges lighting. Caption: "Focus. The track keeps score."
Sequential/interaction: none (continuous motion).
Audio intent: drive and momentum.
Audio-coupled idea: leclerc start jingle ("All right, let's do it.") under the motion.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: dramatic wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Pit radio — 3.5s (14–17.5)
Yellow "PIT LANE ACTIVE" flag, subtitle panel karaoke: "BOX, BOX, BOX." word by word with equalizer bars. Real hamilton.mp3 plays.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 words light sequentially.
Audio intent: the human moment, voices front and center (music ducks).
Audio-coupled idea: word-by-word lighting synced to the real clip.
Music: ducked under radio.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — P1 outro — 2.5s (17.5–20)
Checkered band. "P1 · SESSION COMPLETE" lands on the 18.55 strong cue. URL below. Celebration clip tail.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: payoff, then hard stop at 20s.
Audio-coupled idea: P1 hit lands on strong cue (beat-locked: 18.55s).
Music: climax then cut at 20s.

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic-driving, 110 BPM bed with a real-race climax.
**Audio summary:** beeps build tension, music drives, two real team radios star, everything stops dead at 20 seconds.
